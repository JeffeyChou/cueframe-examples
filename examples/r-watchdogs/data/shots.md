time 0 = film time 18.20.

| # | Film time | Shot | Picture and action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 18.20–22.30 | blame | **Transition at 18.20:** a grid-tile cut. The screen is split by 2 vertical × 2 horizontal lines, the split changes every 0.1 s, and each cell is filled with horizontal lines / squares / vertical lines / dots / diagonal lattice; by 18.45–18.60 it settles into four quadrants. The line-drawn square-cornered word **"BLAME?"** brightens, and at 19.2 it switches to bevelled glyphs. From 18.85 there is a diamond-grid ground with soft central fog. The camera pushes in, anchored on the "?" dot, until the frame reads **"ME?"**. The short dash lands on the letter baseline with small text tight beneath it. At 19.30 a glowing short dash appears. At 19.55, "a small vulnerability inside_" is typed at 42 chars/s, with "vulnerability" on a grey highlight box. From 21.30 "small" dims, and from 21.42 the text is deleted from the left at ~50 chars/s. From 21.10 the letter strokes go out one by one, left to right; the "?" stays until the fade. The shot fades out 22.0–22.3. |
| 6 | 21.75–24.30 | pylons | **three.js:** an array of electricity pylons. The camera starts top-down, then lowers and pushes along the central row of pylons. At 23.05 a ring circles the hero pylon, and the camera pushes in to a truss close-up. The monospace label "_TRANSMISSION_CONTROL_ROOM", on a dark-grey gradient box, continues from where the "ME?" cursor was and drifts with the camera; it is wiped at 24.0. This shot draws *under* the blame shot during their overlap. Use post tone/contrast, not a flat CG look. |
| 7 | 24.35–26.95 | raymond | **"Raymond / Kenney"** in large thin type on striped white boxes, typed letter by letter (the last letter is a grey ghost). **"DISGRUNTLED EMPLOYEE"** in bold on a white box. A portrait grows from small to large, and an X-ray full-body card pops in at 25.5. There is a × grid, a yellow-green file mesh and small text. From 26.0 the whole layer accelerates and whips out to the left. |
| 8 | 26.60–30.60 | virus | A USB icon plugs in from a horizontal line on the left and branches. **"VIRUS"** appears cell by cell as a 5×7 dot matrix: each cell is two short vertical dashes, italic. From 29.0 everything shrinks and fades. |
| 9 | 29.90–33.35 | overloaded | **"OVERLOADED"** as a dot matrix made of tiny text lines, with a file mesh converging to a point. At 31.4 "the grid shutdown" is typed on a white box. From 32.6 everything shrinks to a point, followed by a horizontal flash. |

Style notes: cold near-monochrome HUD on black, cyan/teal accents, Helvetica-like grotesk with
tight negative tracking, monospace typed labels on white boxes, slice-glitch and RGB-split
moments on cuts, film grain.
